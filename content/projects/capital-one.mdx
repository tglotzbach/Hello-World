## Overview

At Capital One, I served as Product Manager for servicing platforms within the card division, focused on building data products that improved recoveries outcomes through smarter decisioning and operational tooling.

## The Challenge

Recoveries operations relied on legacy systems and manual processes that couldn't scale with Capital One's portfolio growth. The opportunity was to build a modern platform layer that turned data into automated, optimized decisioning.

## My Role

- Defined product strategy for the recoveries decisioning platform
- Partnered with data science teams to productize ML models for customer segmentation and contact optimization
- Led engineering teams building APIs, dashboards, and workflow tools used by operations teams
- Managed a roadmap spanning data pipelines, model deployment, and front-end tooling

## Key Outcomes

- Deployed ML-driven decisioning models that improved recovery rates and reduced operational costs
- Launched self-service dashboards enabling operations leaders to monitor performance in real time
- Established platform patterns reused across multiple servicing verticals

## Lessons Learned

Platform thinking means building for the next three teams, not just your own. The best data products disappear into the workflow — they don't ask users to change behavior, they make the right action the default.
