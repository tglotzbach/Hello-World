## Overview

Induck (later Campus Dive) was a consumer product company I co-founded during college. We identified an underserved market, designed and manufactured a physical product, built a brand, and scaled through direct-to-consumer and retail channels — ultimately exiting through acquisition.

## The Journey

- **Ideation & Validation** — Identified market gap, validated demand through pre-sales and campus testing
- **Product Development** — Managed design, prototyping, and manufacturing partnerships
- **Go-to-Market** — Built brand identity, launched e-commerce, and secured retail distribution
- **Growth** — Scaled revenue through digital marketing, campus ambassadors, and event partnerships
- **Exit** — Negotiated and closed acquisition, transitioning the brand and operations

## What I Learned

Building a company from nothing is the most compressed education in product, business, and leadership you can get. Every lesson I apply as a PM — customer empathy, ruthless prioritization, shipping under constraints — traces back to this experience.

## Key Takeaways

- Validate before you build. Pre-sales aren't just revenue — they're conviction.
- Brand is a product decision. How something feels matters as much as what it does.
- Founders think in systems. Every decision cascades. Learn to see the second-order effects.
- Exits are their own product. Packaging a business for acquisition requires the same rigor as packaging a feature for launch.
