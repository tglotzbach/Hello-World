## Overview

Ratcliff RE is an AI-powered platform for real estate investors that automates property analysis, market research, and deal evaluation. As CEO and founder, I own every dimension of the product — from technical architecture to user experience to business model.

## The Stack

- **Backend:** Node.js, Express, TypeScript
- **Frontend:** React with modern component architecture
- **Database:** Supabase (PostgreSQL)
- **AI Layer:** Claude API for analysis, valuation, and natural language interaction
- **Data Sources:** FRED, Census Bureau, Google Places API, county assessor data

## What I'm Building

- Automated property valuation engine combining public data APIs with AI-driven analysis
- Market comparison tools that surface hyperlocal trends and investment signals
- Natural language interface for querying deals, markets, and portfolio performance
- Investor-grade reports generated from raw data through multi-step AI pipelines

## Why This Matters

Real estate investing is drowning in data but starving for insight. Most investors rely on gut feel, spreadsheets, and outdated comps. Ratcliff RE brings the analytical rigor of institutional investing to individual operators — powered by AI that actually understands real estate.

## Product Philosophy

Build the tool I'd want to use. Ship fast, learn faster. Let AI handle the tedious analysis so investors can focus on judgment and relationships.
