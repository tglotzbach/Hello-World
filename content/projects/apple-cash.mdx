## Overview

Apple Cash is the peer-to-peer payments and spending platform embedded within Apple Wallet, powered by Green Dot's banking infrastructure. As a Product Manager at Green Dot, I led critical workstreams across this partnership — one of the most visible embedded finance programs in the world.

## The Challenge

Operating at the intersection of two highly regulated, highly scaled organizations required navigating complex compliance requirements, multi-stakeholder alignment, and platform-level architectural decisions — all while maintaining the seamless user experience Apple demands.

## My Role

- Drove product strategy for key Apple Cash features across payments, compliance, and infrastructure
- Partnered with Apple's product and engineering teams to align roadmaps and technical specifications
- Managed regulatory requirements across money transmission, KYC/AML, and consumer protection
- Coordinated cross-functional execution across engineering, compliance, operations, and partner teams

## Key Outcomes

- Scaled infrastructure to support millions of active users across the Apple ecosystem
- Shipped critical compliance and feature enhancements on time within Apple's release cycles
- Built frameworks for partner communication and decision-making that reduced alignment friction

## Lessons Learned

Embedded finance at scale is a systems problem. The hardest work isn't building features — it's designing the organizational and technical interfaces that let two very different companies ship as one.
