## Overview

When I decided to explore new opportunities, I didn't want to spend hours scrolling job boards. Instead, I built a multi-agent automation system using Claude that screens, scores, and surfaces the best-fit roles from a curated list of 400+ target companies.

## How It Works

The system uses Claude's Dispatch and Cowork capabilities to orchestrate multiple specialized bots:

1. **Sourcing Bot** — Monitors job boards and company career pages for new PM/product roles
2. **Screening Bot** — Reads job descriptions and extracts structured data (level, domain, tech stack, location)
3. **Scoring Bot** — Evaluates each role against a 6-dimension match rubric calibrated to my experience and preferences
4. **Summary Bot** — Generates daily digests ranked by match score with key highlights

## The 6-Dimension Rubric

- **Domain fit** — Payments, fintech, AI, proptech
- **Level match** — Senior/Staff PM seniority
- **Technical depth** — Requires hands-on technical fluency
- **Company stage** — Growth-stage or established with startup energy
- **Location/remote** — DMV area or remote-friendly
- **Mission alignment** — Building something that matters

## Key Outcomes

- Processed 400+ companies and thousands of job postings automatically
- Reduced daily job search time from 2+ hours to 10 minutes of reviewing scored summaries
- Discovered high-fit roles I would have missed through manual searching

## Why It Matters

This project demonstrates AI-native operator thinking: instead of using AI as a tool, I designed a system where AI agents collaborate to solve a multi-step workflow end-to-end.
