## How It Works

This system uses Claude Cowork to turn raw experience and ideas into polished LinkedIn content:

1. **Input Layer** — I feed the system themes, experiences, and rough ideas from my work in fintech, AI, and product management
2. **Draft Generation** — Claude generates multiple draft angles, each grounded in specific experiences rather than generic advice
3. **Voice Calibration** — The system is tuned to my writing voice — direct, specific, and grounded in real work rather than platitudes
4. **Editing Pipeline** — Drafts go through structured revision to tighten language, sharpen points, and ensure authenticity

The goal isn't to automate writing — it's to automate the hardest part of writing: getting started. The system surfaces drafts that feel like mine, so I can focus on editing and publishing rather than staring at a blank page.
