## How It Works

This automation uses Claude's Dispatch and Cowork features to orchestrate a pipeline of specialized AI agents. Each bot handles one step of the workflow:

1. **Sourcing** — Pulls fresh listings from target companies
2. **Screening** — Extracts structured data from job descriptions
3. **Scoring** — Rates each role on domain fit, level match, technical depth, company stage, location, and mission alignment
4. **Summarizing** — Delivers a ranked daily digest

The system processes hundreds of listings and reduces daily search time from hours to minutes. It's designed to be recalibrated as priorities shift — the rubric is the product, and the bots are the execution layer.
