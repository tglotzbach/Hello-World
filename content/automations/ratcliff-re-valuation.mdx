## How It Works

The valuation engine is a multi-step data pipeline that feeds into Claude for AI-powered analysis:

1. **Data Collection** — Pulls property data, market indicators (FRED), demographic data (Census Bureau), and local context (Google Places API)
2. **Data Enrichment** — Normalizes and combines data sources into a structured property profile
3. **AI Analysis** — Claude processes the enriched profile to generate valuation estimates, risk assessments, and investment recommendations
4. **Report Generation** — Outputs investor-grade reports with comparable analysis, market context, and projected returns

This isn't a simple chatbot wrapper — it's a structured pipeline where AI handles the analytical heavy lifting while maintaining the rigor investors need to make real decisions.
